About Us

A vibrant, Spirit-led Christian ministry committed to spreading the message of Jesus Christ across the world.

Our story

Carriers of God’s fire

Chariot of Fire World Outreach (COFWO) is a vibrant, Spirit-led Christian ministry committed to spreading the message of Jesus Christ across the world, transforming lives through the power of the Holy Spirit, and raising a generation of believers equipped to manifest God’s glory in every area of life.

Born out of a divine mandate to ignite revival and restore destinies, COFWO serves as a vessel of transformation—touching lives, empowering believers, and proclaiming the gospel with passion and purpose. We believe that every individual is called to be a carrier of God’s fire and an instrument of change in their generation.

Our passion

We are consumed with a passion to see the fire of God rekindled in this generation. Through fervent prayer, deep worship, the teaching of God’s Word, and the demonstration of His power. Our values burn brightly as the flame that guides our conduct, ministry, and service to humanity.

Our Mission, Goal & Vision

01

Our Mission

Setting captives free by the Spirit of Prophecy.

Our mission is to bring deliverance, healing, and restoration through the power of the Holy Spirit, raising people who hear God clearly and walk in spiritual authority and freedom in Christ.

02

Our Goal

To see lives, people, cities, nations touched by the fire of the Holy Spirit.

Our goal is to spread revival and transformation across communities and nations, igniting hearts with passion for God and empowering believers to live a life of purpose, power, and holiness.

03

Our Vision

To see the move of the Spirit in all lives and nations of the world.

Our vision is a global revival where the presence and power of God transform individuals, families, cities, and nations, raising burning and shining lights for Christ across the world.
