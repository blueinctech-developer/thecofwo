Privacy Policy

How The Chariot of Fire World Outreach handles personal information on this website.

This policy explains what information we collect through thecofwo.org, why we collect it and how we look after it. Last updated: 3 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by The Chariot of Fire World Outreach (“COFWO”, “we”, “us”). You can contact us about this policy or your information at info@thecofwo.org.

Information we collect

Contact form

When you send us a message we receive your first and last name, email address, subject and message. We use these only to reply to you and to provide the prayer, guidance or information you ask for.

Newsletter

When you sign up to our newsletter we receive your email address. We use it to send church news, events and encouragement. You can ask us to remove you at any time by emailing us.

Services that process your information

  • Web3Forms receives form submissions and forwards them to our email inbox.
  • Our website host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as The Gathering website. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

How long we keep your information

We keep messages only as long as needed to respond and follow up with you, and newsletter addresses until you ask us to remove them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to hearing from us. Email info@thecofwo.org and we will respond as soon as we can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
