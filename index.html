Who we are

Igniting Hearts, Transforming Nations

The Chariot of Fire World Outreach (COFWO) is a dynamic ministry dedicated to carrying the fire of God's love, power, and salvation to the ends of the earth.

We believe in a present, active, and powerful God whose Spirit is moving mightily in our generation. Our mission is to champion this move, equip the saints, and see lives transformed by the glory of God.

What drives us

Our Mission, Goal & Vision

01

Our Mission

Setting captives free by the Spirit of Prophecy.

Our mission is to bring deliverance, healing, and restoration through the power of the Holy Spirit, raising people who hear God clearly and walk in spiritual authority and freedom in Christ.

02

Our Goal

To see lives, people, cities, nations touched by the fire of the Holy Spirit.

Our goal is to spread revival and transformation across communities and nations, igniting hearts with passion for God and empowering believers to live a life of purpose, power, and holiness.

03

Our Vision

To see the move of the Spirit in all lives and nations of the world.

Our vision is a global revival where the presence and power of God transform individuals, families, cities, and nations, raising burning and shining lights for Christ across the world.

Our annual event

The Gathering: Event Highlights

Experience the unforgettable highlights from The Gathering, our annual COFWO event that unites believers from across the nation and beyond. Relive the powerful worship moments, life-changing encounters, and transformational messages that stirred hearts and strengthened faith.

This yearly gathering draws worshippers into a weekend of deep spiritual renewal, fellowship, and divine encounter—an atmosphere where God moves, lives are lifted, and destinies are realigned.
